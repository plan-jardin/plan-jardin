Privacy

Your data stays yours.

plan jardin does not collect, receive, or store personal data. We do not operate a backend. When you use Google Drive or weather features, your browser connects directly to the relevant provider, which may process information needed to provide its service.

Last updated 02 November 2026

Google Drive

If you connect Google Drive, Google handles sign-in and authorization. plan jardin requests access only to its own hidden appDataFolder. The app sends your saved data directly from your browser to Google Drive; plan jardin does not receive or keep a copy. The access token is held in browser memory to make those requests. Revoking access stops future access but may not delete data already stored in your Drive. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Weather and browser storage

Place searches are sent directly to Open-Meteo's geocoding service and include the search text. Weather requests include the selected coordinates and dates. Map tiles and reverse-geocoding requests for a selected map point are sent directly to OpenStreetMap services. Weather responses may be cached in your browser's local storage; that cache is not sent to plan jardin. You can clear it through your browser's site-data settings. Open-Meteo and OpenStreetMap may process technical request data under their own policies.

Hosting and third parties

The site is delivered by GitHub Pages. Google, Open-Meteo, and GitHub may process technical or account information as needed to provide their services, under their own privacy policies, including GitHub's Privacy Statement. plan jardin does not use advertising trackers or analytics.

Your choices

Connecting Google Drive is optional. You can revoke plan jardin's Google access in your Google Account permissions. Data already saved in Drive remains subject to your Google account and Drive controls.
